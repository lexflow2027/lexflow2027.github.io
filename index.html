01 / About

About the Workshop

Co-located with ACM IUI 2027 —
February 8-11, 2027, Helsinki, Finland

LEXFLOW 2027 explores how intelligent user interfaces can support people in understanding, supervising and steering agentic legal reasoning workflows.

As AI systems become increasingly embedded in legal research, drafting, compliance, fact verification and other forms of legal decision support, users no longer interact only with isolated model outputs. Legal work may unfold across multiple steps, tools, evidence sources, intermediate decisions and moments of human intervention - increasingly involving multiple specialised AI agents coordinating with one another.

Legal professionals remain responsible for evaluating evidence, interpreting uncertainty, identifying errors and deciding whether an AI-supported conclusion can be trusted or acted upon. Yet many current legal AI interfaces remain output-centred, offering limited visibility into task decomposition, consulted sources, intermediate choices, uncertainty, agent contributions or opportunities for meaningful human input.

LEXFLOW asks how legal AI can move beyond producing answers towards supporting process-level supervision. We are interested in interaction mechanisms that make plans, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, intermediate reasoning, agent coordination and intervention points visible and actionable.

The workshop brings together researchers and practitioners from HCI, IUI, AI and Law, legal AI, Human-Centered AI and related communities. Through paper and demo presentations, co-design and collaborative synthesis, we will work towards reusable design patterns, evaluation questions, workflow representations and a shared research agenda for legal AI that is more inspectable, steerable, verifiable, contestable and accountable.

02 / Call for papers

Call for Papers

We welcome original submissions that have not been simultaneously submitted to another journal, conference, or workshop. LEXFLOW 2027 invites contributions from researchers and practitioners across HCI, IUI, AI and Law, legal AI, and human-centered AI.

Submissions should clearly articulate their relevance to agentic legal reasoning and the implications of the work for the design, evaluation or understanding of intelligent interfaces that support human oversight of AI-assisted legal reasoning.

Short Papers
5-9 pages. Emerging research, preliminary findings, design provocations, conceptual perspectives, case studies or position statements.

Regular Research Papers
More than 10 pages. Substantial empirical studies, systems, interaction designs, technical approaches, evaluation methods or conceptual contributions.

Demo Papers
5-9 pages. Interactive systems, prototypes, tools or agentic legal AI workflows.

04 / Topics

List of Topics

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Agentic legal reasoning workflows
  • Human-AI legal reasoning and collaboration
  • Intelligent user interfaces for legal AI
  • Process-level supervision and human oversight
  • Inspectable and transparent reasoning processes
  • Steering and intervention in AI-assisted legal workflows
  • Evidence provenance and source verification
  • Uncertainty communication in legal AI
  • Contestability and correction of AI-supported reasoning
  • Accountability and responsibility in human-AI legal work
  • Multi-agent coordination in legal reasoning systems
  • Workflow decomposition, planning, and intermediate decisions
  • Interaction design for legal AI systems
  • Evaluation methods for agentic and intelligent legal interfaces
  • Reusable design patterns for human-centered legal AI
